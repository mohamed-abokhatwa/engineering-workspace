Connection & privacy

Outlook on this computerOn a Mac, Outlook (Legacy); on a PC, classic Outlook — read directly, nothing to set up, no approval to ask for. The new Outlook cannot share its mailbox with any program; the page says so and shows the way round.
Your account, your sign-inPer-user Microsoft sign-in via the official device-code flow — at microsoft.com, never in the app.
When IT must approveCompany tenants sometimes answer “Need admin approval”. The sign-in window then shows the exact permissions (read-only) and a ready message for IT with the approval link — one approval, then everyone in the company signs in.
Thunderbird as the readerRead your mail in Thunderbird (free; Microsoft 365, Google, any IMAP account) and a small connector hands it to the workspace on this computer only. On a PC the app registers the connector for you; Thunderbird installs it at its next start.
Per-user encrypted tokensNo user — and no API — can read another's mailbox. Ever.
Reads and drafts onlyThe workspace never sends mail, never edits your mailbox, calendar or notes.

Follow-ups

Track what THEY promised youOne press on a received e-mail puts the sender's promise on the same board, chased with the same patience — a commitment made to you is a commitment either way.
Automatic trackingEvery real ask you send becomes a tracked thread — invites, auto-replies and no-reply robots filtered out.
Working-day patienceChoose the patience (e.g. 3 working days); weekends — YOUR weekend — never count.
Reply detectionReplies found in the inbox automatically close the wait; a resend after a reply reopens it.
Drafted chasersWhen patience runs out, a polite chaser is already written — you send it, or not.
Quiet threads + reopenSilence a thread without losing it; every item links back to the original conversation.
Who owes youEach thread names the person and the days — the chase list writes itself.

Calendar & meetings

Today, unmistakableThe calendar marks today so a week view never confuses you.
Meeting prep packsDocuments + open register items + recent mail on the subject — deterministic, cached 7 days, saved to your files.
Minutes from notesRough notes become formal minutes — attendees, decisions, actions with owners.
Notes → actions (Mac)Apple Notes on a Mac workspace surface as structured briefs — handwriting included — with each action producible in one press.

Triage & reading

AI inbox triageUrgent-action / action / awaiting-others / info / noise — with a one-line reason each.
Letters keep their numberingA consultant's five-point letter arrives as five numbered points, not five items all called “1” — the reader takes the rich body and keeps Word's own list numbers.
The inbox briefA composed morning read of the mailbox: act on these, worth knowing, waiting on — each line linked to its thread.
Attachments in placeA message's attachments appear as chips and open right there — fetched on demand into a small self-pruning cache, never stored twice.
The whole thread, then a summaryRead a conversation from day one in one reader, and press once for a streamed summary of what has been agreed, refused and left open.
Handwritten notes, read backApple Notes handwriting is transcribed on the server and comes back as a numbered brief — each point extractable into an action the workspace produces.
Draft repliesA reply drafted with project facts searched from the knowledge base — never sent by the app.
Mail into the recordIngested correspondence lands in the knowledge base folder, searchable like everything else.
