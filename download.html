Install it on your machine. Your documents never leave it.

The complete workspace — search, registers, the outbound check, the studios — installed on your own computer. Every install starts a 14-day full evaluation on your own documents; an activation code by e-mail continues it. Nothing here is uploaded anywhere, ever.

Download for Windows

Unsigned while our signing certificate is in progress — if SmartScreen asks, choose More info → Run anyway. The wizard checks your machine before installing and needs no internet during setup. Prefer the Store? The same app installs and updates itself from there.

macOS

Installer package · 1.7 GB · Apple Silicon, macOS 13+
Download for Mac

A native installer wizard. First launch: right-click the app → Open (once) while our signing certificate is in progress.

1 · Install
Everything ships inside the installer — both search-quality models, readers, the drawing converter, OCR. No downloads mid-setup, which is why the file is large.
2 · First run
A 15-second machine check, then your one-time setup code creates the admin account.
3 · Connect a folder
Point it at the project folders you already keep — indexed in place, nothing copied.
4 · Activate
Request a code when the evaluation ends — until then the full product is yours to judge.
Verify your download

Two checksums, one command.

Every website installer is published with its SHA-256 checksum. Compare it with the file you downloaded — the two must match exactly. The Store package carries Microsoft’s signature and needs no manual check. The same values are in SHA256SUMS.txt and checksums.json.

What changed · r173 → r183

Since the last website release.

Notes from your iPad, on the PCA Notes page of its own: quick notes next to the work, and the pages you write in OneNote on a Windows PC or Apple Notes on a Mac — handwriting from an iPad or phone included, recognised as text — each one turned into actions, a follow-up or a calendar entry with one press. Nothing to register, nothing for IT to approve; the app only reads.
The company licence, end to endOne installer for everyone: a personal key keeps it to one person, a company key opens users and roles, the access gate, remote access and the client portal, and counts its seats (portal-only accounts are free). Every role was walked against every route and the sign-off chain by five different people before this release.
A quieter first dayThe first read of a busy mailbox rings once with the totals instead of once per old thread, and the evaluation is described as it really is: fourteen days from first launch, then read-only, nothing deleted.
Reads for one personOn a personal licence the app never talks about other users, administrators or sharing — every page, the settings and the manual are worded for the one engineer who owns it. The company licence keeps the full multi-user text.
Mail on a PC, three waysThe Mail page reads classic Outlook on the computer directly, your own Microsoft account, or Thunderbird through a small connector that the app sets up for you. When a company mailbox needs a one-time approval, the sign-in window shows exactly what to send IT.
Two search-quality tiersFast for everyday recall, Deep for large technical libraries — both included, chosen for you from the machine's memory on the first index, switchable later without re-reading a single document.
Drawings out of the boxDWG and DXF drawings read on Windows with the bundled converter — nothing to install, nothing to configure.
Orders that survive the pageAsk a question, start a check, run a rewrite, then move on — the answer is waiting when you come back. Search boxes, filters and drafts remember themselves too.
Calmer, more honestOne quiet start-up curtain instead of a flash of screens; a start-up that waits for a slow server instead of freezing; PC-appropriate wording throughout; the About page says what the app is and who makes it.
AI that answersAssistant, mail triage, technical writing and the outbound check are resilient to crowded AI providers, use the right model tier per task, and label honestly which one did the work.
