Download · r210 · updated 16 September 2026

Install it on your machine. Your documents never leave it.

The complete workspace — search, registers, the outbound check, the studios — installed on your own computer. Every install starts a 14-day full evaluation on your own documents; an activation code by e-mail continues it. Nothing here is uploaded anywhere, ever.

On Windows, the Microsoft Store is the simplest route and the one we would take: Microsoft signs and delivers the package, and it updates itself. The direct installer is there for the machines and the companies where the Store is not an option.

Windows

Installed and updated by the Store · Windows 10/11, 64-bit

The Store is the easiest way in: Microsoft signs and delivers the package, so there is no security warning to click past, and updates arrive by themselves. It is the same product, with the same 14-day evaluation.

Or the direct installer · 1.7 GB · for a company that blocks the Store, or an offline machine
Download the installer instead

Windows shows a SmartScreen notice for any installer it has not seen enough times yet, ours included — choose More info → Run anyway. The checksum below is there so you can confirm the file is exactly the one we published. The wizard checks your machine before installing and needs no internet during setup.

Installer package · 1.7 GB · Apple Silicon, macOS 13+
Download for Mac

A native installer wizard. On first launch, right-click the app and choose Open once — macOS asks this of any app it has not been told to trust yet. The checksum below confirms the file is exactly the one we published.

Everything ships inside the installer — both search-quality models, readers, the drawing converter, OCR. No downloads mid-setup, which is why the file is large.

Point it at the project folders you already keep — indexed in place, nothing copied.

Two checksums, one command.

Every website installer is published with its SHA-256 checksum. Compare it with the file you downloaded — the two must match exactly. The Store package carries Microsoft’s signature and needs no manual check. The same values are in SHA256SUMS.txt and checksums.json.

What changed · r200 → r210

Since the last website release.

Reports that prove what they sayAsk for a report that cites your documents and you now get the evidence itself, not a page number. Every finding is printed beside the exact wording it rests on and beside a picture of that page with those words marked, so whoever reads it checks the citation by looking at it rather than taking it on trust. Every quoted line is checked against the document first: if the wording is not really there the report is not produced at all, and you are told what the page actually says. A page number that is one out is corrected for you, and the report says so.
Every page teaches the whole of itselfOpen a page’s lesson and it walks that page completely: every tab opened in turn, every control moved to, pointed at and explained, in English and Arabic. Twenty-nine lessons, 656 steps — the studios, the client portal and the application’s own frame have one for the first time, Settings walks all seven of its sections and the Library all nine of its tabs. A control that only appears once you have work in the workspace is still taught, on the place it will appear, with a line saying when it arrives. Nothing is skipped and nothing is left unexplained.
Drawings, measured — not only countedA DWG or DXF take-off now reads the lines and not only the blocks. Pipe and duct runs are measured per layer, a bend is measured as a true arc rather than as the straight line across it — a 90° bend measured as its chord is eleven per cent short, and a take-off made of bends is then eleven per cent short everywhere — and every total can be traced back to the exact entities that produced it. Hatches, dimensions and solids are noted and never measured, because a hatch boundary is not a pipe run and counting it is how a take-off silently doubles.
What leaves a studio looks like the studioClouds, callouts, stamps, dimensions, text and highlights now export in the colour, the size and the orientation they had on the sheet — in Acrobat, in a browser, in Preview, on a sheet the drawing office turned sideways. A highlight is one band the width of the words, in the colour you chose. A stamp’s text sits centred in its box. The Word markup report carries a true picture of each mark rather than an approximation of one.
The studios do what their buttons sayFit fits the page to the space that is actually there. The magnet holds to line ends, midpoints and along a line, and says so when a page has nothing to snap to. The page strip hides and comes back, the side panel can be reopened after it is closed, and Print prints. The CAD studio got the same pass: white paper, working snap, a Fit that uses both dimensions, and layers you turn off staying off in every export.
An opened e-mail says who else received itTo and Cc on every message, on every mail route — the mailbox open on this computer, Thunderbird, or your own Microsoft account — folded after six names with the rest one press away. Knowing who else was on a letter is half of reading it.
The Library says what it countsCoverage used to show a percentage per folder that nobody could name. It now leads with the figure that answers the question: files read out of files found, folder by folder, climbing to a hundred per cent as indexing works through and moving while you watch it. The older figure is still there, named for what it measures — of the pages actually opened, the share whose words could be read — so a folder of scans waiting on a longer run is told apart from a folder that is simply not finished yet.
Notes written on a PC reach the workspaceOneNote installed from the Microsoft Store used to be unreachable no matter how it was opened. It is reached now the way that works on Windows, with two further routes behind it, and the page says which one answered — so a note written on a tablet, handwriting included, arrives where the rest of the project is.
A sheet exports as it was drawnA drawing the office turned sideways exports the right way up, undo and redo keep their place through a save, and a file you pinned stays pinned. The memory discipline behind the studios was rebuilt at the same time, which is what ended the crash dialogs on long drawing sets.
The inbox brief is always a briefThe morning brief is read for sense before it is shown: if what comes back is not a real reading of your inbox, it is asked for again and, failing that, the triage stands on its own rather than showing you something hollow. The mail page also names exactly which mailbox it is watching instead of guessing at the machine it is running on.
A clock that keeps its placeLeave a page while the assistant is working and come back: the elapsed time carries on from where it truly is, instead of starting again from zero. Small, and it was the difference between believing the number and ignoring it.
A highlight that survives leaving the appA band drawn over a paragraph of a specification leaves the studio as one highlight in the colour it was given — the same in every reader, on drawings and on text alike.
