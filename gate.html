Before you send

Document review normally reads what ARRIVES. This reads what is about to LEAVE. The asymmetry is the whole point: by the time a request has gone out, the cost of the mistake inside it is already fixed.

2:48 · WATCHThe outbound check that reads your specification before the Engineer does
The outbound-check film: a draft read against its own sources.

Two speeds, deliberately

The instant pass runs on the wording alone. It costs nothing, returns in a second, and is safe to run while somebody is still typing. The full check spends real effort: it goes and retrieves the specification sections the draft leans on and reads them whole, which is the only way to catch a contradiction nobody remembered.

Instant: editions, absolute claims, undeclared compliance, self-contradicting limits, missing response dates
Full: the governing sections retrieved and read end to end, with the contradicting clause quoted
Half an answer always beats a spinner — instant findings survive if the full pass fails
One overstated compliance claimand the reviewer reads everything else you wrote with suspicion.
One deviation you did not declareand it comes back, and it is remembered next time.
One edition the contract does not bindand the rejection is a technicality that still costs a full cycle.
No required response dateand the answer arrives whenever it happens to suit.
EditionsWhich edition of each code the contract binds, which edition is actually on the shelf, and where the two disagree.
ConflictsTwo requirements about the same component that cannot both be satisfied — a US listing here, a British standard there.
Acceptable makersEvery clause that already names manufacturers, with what their products must satisfy. Arguments you already own.
Authority fileWhat the contract requires of the code authority, what has been issued for its review, and what is missing.

The specification, read against itself

These run once per specification revision rather than once per question, and they are the reason the check can be specific. A conflict found at tender is a priced qualification. The same conflict found at procurement is a delay and an argument. And a clause that already names your manufacturer as acceptable is the strongest sentence your letter can carry — most of them are never used, because nobody knows the clause is there.

Deterministic where it can be: an answer always traces to the sentence it came from
Runs on your own server — no per-question cost, nothing leaves

What the change obliges elsewhere

Substitutions are never local. Change one fitting's thread and you have quietly obliged new couplings, nozzles, reducers, caps and the fire authority's inlets — none of which appears in the request that started it. Mixed threads in a tower is a hazard discovered at commissioning, which is the worst possible moment to discover anything. The workspace walks the dependency and lists what else the change touches: other components, approved submittals, procured items, drawings, other trades.

And what the Engineer already said

A project carries years of clarifications, each with a written response from the party who will answer the next one. That is institutional memory sitting as files, findable only by whoever was there at the time. The workspace turns it into an answer: this has been ruled on, here is the ruling, here is how it was reasoned — and it predicts the likely reply from what this Engineer actually wrote, not from a generic model of consultants.

A response from your own side is labelled as such, never passed off as authority
Survives staff turnover — the value compounds the longer a project runs
The other half

And it proves you were right.

Everything above stops a mistake leaving. The record is what protects you when the answer is slow.

The response recordHow long this project actually takes to answer, measured from clarifications carrying both dates — median, ninetieth percentile and worst. A number measured from the project beats a period quoted from the conditions.
The evidence chainFor any one reference: issued, allowed, answered or not, and what it consumed. The exhibit itself, not a paragraph claiming one exists.
Notices on recordWhich entitlements were noticed and when — the part people forget until it matters.
The handover dossierCloseout obligations counted from the specification clauses themselves and set against what is actually filed, long before the month the building is due.
Bilingual outputThe same document in Arabic and English block for block, with clause numbers, standard references and quoted contract text left in English — what a regional reviewer actually wants.
Cited historyEvery answer the workspace gave, with the pages it came from. Your defensible trail.
And beyond the paper

The building, and the company.

Two places the same evidence goes further: out to the site, and up to the head office.

Is this what was approved?Photograph an installed nameplate and it is read against the approved submittal for that component — the manufacturer, the model, the listings. Not a checklist asking whether it looks neat.
Honest about a photographIt reports that a listing mark is not visible in the image. It never claims an item is unlisted, and an unreadable photo produces one finding about the photo.
Say it onceSpeak a snag while walking and it becomes the right record — defect to the issues register, question to an RFI — with the references you said and the sheet you had open. The listening runs on the phone, so it costs nothing.
Across every jobWhich consultant actually answers, which return reason repeats on four projects, and which specification conflict is already sitting in the next tender document.
The test we invite

Do not trust the demo. Try to break it.

A polished demo proves nothing. The honest test of a document engine is a trick question, asked on a real specification. Bring a sanitized project to your trial and put these three traps in front of it:

01

The conflicting clause

Ask about a requirement where two clauses of the same specification disagree. A keyword tool returns the sentence that agrees with you. The check is built to read the section whole and surface the one that does not.

02

The wrong edition

Cite a section number from a code edition your contract does not bind. The editions register exists exactly for this: the rejection that looks like a technicality, caught before it costs a full cycle.

03

The ruling that changed everything

Ask a question whose answer a previous Engineer response quietly reversed. The precedent search answers from your own correspondence, with the letter and the date.

If it misses one, tell us exactly what it missed. That report is worth more to us than a compliment, and it is how the engine got this far.

Hand it one of your own requests.
