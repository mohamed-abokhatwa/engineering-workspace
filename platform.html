More pillars

ProgrammeThe Primavera P6 programme imported and viewed beside delivery — plan and record in one place, with a three-week look-ahead and the critical path.
Before the pourThe concrete pouring plan held against the shop drawings and submittals that must be approved before each date, flagged the days early you choose. The whole board →
Handover dossierCloseout obligations counted from the specification clauses themselves and set against what is actually filed — long before the month the building is due.
Scope & VariationsA variation register plus a commercial radar that screens consultant comments for hidden entitlement.
ReviewsAI document review against the specifications — comment drafts with citations, an engineer approves.
On siteSpeak a snag and it files itself as the right record; site photos checked against the approved submittal, not a generic checklist.
Before you sendThe outbound gate: every draft checked against the clauses it relies on before it leaves.
CompareAny two document revisions overlaid — removed in red, added in teal — beyond the automatic workflow compares.
Across projectsThe head-office view over every workspace project: turnaround, return reasons, repeat conflicts.
My NotesA personal notes board, per user, next to the work.
History & JobsEvery background job visible with progress; every action in the audit trail.

Workspace

Multi-projectEach project keeps its own knowledge base, index, registers and workflows; switching re-points everything.
Project archiveRetire a project to a dated archive folder — documents, index and a database export — restorable.
Notifications centreEvery completion, arrival and escalation — in-app, and to your phone if you enable push.
Client portal, company-controlledA client sign-in reaches one read-only progress page and nothing else — and YOUR company decides exactly what it shows: every section, the plan comparison, individual disciplines, the transmittal count, even the welcome note. A figure you switch off is never sent, not merely hidden.
Arabic-readyThe interface ships with an Arabic RTL option; the engine reads Arabic documents natively.

Trust

Sign-in alwaysNo anonymous access; rate-limited logins; two-factor enforced for administrators.
Project-pinned accountsTrial and demo users locked to one project — your live projects do not exist for them.
Roles enforced at the server, not the screenFive roles — owner, admin, engineer, management, client. What a role may not see is never sent to their browser at all, so a typed address reveals nothing. Audited by signing in as each one and looking.
The owner's own machine stays the owner'sMail, calendar and notes read from the computer the workspace runs on belong to the owner alone · not to the admin role. An administrator can run the whole company here and never see them.
Encrypted per-user secretsMail sign-ins and AI keys encrypted at rest, per person.
Signed updatesUpdate packages must carry a valid signature to install.
BackupsScheduled local backups plus an encrypted nightly cloud backup of the app record.
Audit trailLogins, changes, approvals, substitutions, escalations — permanent and searchable.

Connections

Outlook / Microsoft 365Per-user sign-in for mail, calendar and OneDrive — tokens encrypted per user.
Autodesk Construction CloudEach user signs in to their OWN ACC account on Autodesk's site; the workspace never holds anyone's Autodesk password.
PortalsSupervised fetch from consultant portals; downloads land in an intake queue.
FeedsToken-secured calendar and BI feeds for the tools your office already uses.
MCP both waysYour AI agents can plug INTO the workspace, and the workspace can call external agent tools — the AI layer multiplies.
AutomationsHourly sweeps (follow-ups, escalations) and a weekly digest — visible, adjustable, off-switchable.
