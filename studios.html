The Studios

Drawings are where engineering actually happens. The Studios bring markup, CAD and comparison into the same workspace as everything else — no desktop licences, no exports.

Markup tools

ShapesRectangle, ellipse, cloud (real revision clouds), line, arrow, polyline, polygon, freehand ink, highlight.
Words on the sheetText boxes, leader callouts, sticky notes — with font size and colour control.
StampsAPPROVED, REJECTED, REVISED, FOR REVIEW, AS-BUILT, VOID — placed and scaled like any markup.
Apple Pencil on the iPadThe Pencil draws, a finger still pans and pinches, and the heel of your hand is ignored — so a sheet can be marked up on the tablet you already carry round the site. Pressure changes the weight of the stroke.
Photo pinsAttach a photo to a location on the sheet — site condition, meet the drawing.
Colour disciplineSix-colour palette so each reviewer or topic keeps its own colour.

Measure & count

Calibrated measurementDraw a known length once — every measurement after that is in real units.
AreasPolygon areas with the value written on the region.
CountsNumbered count sequences per colour — sprinkler heads, sockets, fixtures — totals kept per sheet.
