Engineering Workspace / Security
Security & architecture

Your record, your hardware,
our discipline.

Engineering correspondence is confidential and contractual. This page says plainly how the workspace protects it — and, just as plainly, what is architecture and what is not yet an independent audit.

The architecture

Where everything lives.

Data on your infrastructureDocuments, the search index and the database live on the machine your company operates. Indexing, OCR and search run locally; a thousand questions cost nothing and leave nothing behind in a vendor cloud.
One encrypted doorRemote access passes through an encrypted tunnel with an edge allow-list; there is no anonymous surface. Sign-in is always required, and failed logins are rate-limited and alerted.
Two-factor for administratorsAdmin sessions can install updates, so a password alone is not accepted: administrators must enrol an authenticator app before their first session.
Roles that fence, server-sideOwner, admin, engineer, viewer, client, management. A client sign-in reaches one read-only portal route and nothing else — enforced in the API, not hidden in the interface. What the company switches off in the portal is never even sent, and an address typed by hand reveals nothing a role may not have.
Walked, not assumedThe boundary is not a claim on a page: before each release the whole product is walked twice, once as an ordinary engineer and once as an administrator who is not the owner, watching every request the browser makes. Anything a role should not see and still receives is a defect, and is fixed before the release ships.
The operator's own machine stays theirsMail, calendar and notes read from the computer the workspace runs on belong to the person who owns that workspace — not to the administrator role. A customer's IT administrator can run their whole company in it and never reach them, unless the owner deliberately widens that.
Per-user encrypted secretsMail sign-ins and AI keys are stored encrypted per person. No user, and no API, can read another's mailbox or key.
Signed updates onlyUpdate packages must carry a valid signature from the seller's key to install. A tampered package refuses itself.
Licences without a data pathInstalled copies validate a signed licence file locally and renew it with a small daily check-in that carries version, machine code and document counts — never content. If the licence service is unreachable the app keeps working through a generous grace window, and an expired licence turns the workspace read-only; nothing is ever disabled or deleted remotely. A site tool must never brick mid-project.
Backups, two layersScheduled local backups of the record, plus an encrypted nightly backup of the application record to storage the operator chooses. Restores are part of the design, not an afterthought.
An audit trail that remembersLogins, changes, approvals, substitutions, escalations and deletions — who did what, permanently on the record.
Trials cannot touch youTrial and demo accounts are pinned to a demo project. Live projects do not exist for them.
Honesty clause

What this page is, and is not.

Everything above is how the system is built and operated today. It is not yet an independent certification, and we will not pretend otherwise. Before the first multi-company rollout, the roadmap includes an independent penetration test, and practices are run ISO 27001-aligned ahead of any formal programme.
