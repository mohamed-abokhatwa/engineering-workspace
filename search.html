Reading

Every format that mattersPDF, Word, Excel, scanned images, drawings — read into one knowledge base.
OCR, English + ArabicOld letters and stamped drawings become searchable text — the archive stops being a blind spot.
Watched foldersNew arrivals index themselves; the knowledge base is as fresh as the last document.
Per-project isolationEach project keeps its own index — switching projects switches the brain.
Drawings, out of the boxDWG and DXF drawings are read with a bundled converter — no extra install, on Mac or PC.

Finding

Hybrid searchMeaning-based + keyword, together, reranked — finds 'pump clearance' when the letter says 'access around the skid'.
Every-mention sweepExhaustive mode: every document that mentions a term, with true counts.
Ask with citationsPlain-language answers composed from the evidence, each claim cited (document, p.X).
Citations libraryReal answers auto-capture their citations for reuse in letters and reports.
⌘K everywhereThe palette searches and jumps from any page.
Two quality tiers, your choiceFast for everyday recall; Deep for large technical libraries. The app reads the machine's memory and picks one for the first index — switch later and the index rebuilds from what was already read, never from the documents again.
Orders survive the pageAsk, leave, come back: the answer is waiting. Nothing you start is lost to a page change.
