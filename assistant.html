What the assistant can actually do — tool by tool.

Evidence

Search & sweepHybrid search plus exhaustive every-mention sweeps across the indexed project.
Read any documentOpens the indexed text of a document, page by page.
Look at the sheetRenders a drawing page and READS it — scanned title blocks, legends, symbols — never guesses.
Lift printed tablesCopies a real grid off a PDF page cell by cell — no retyping, no drifted numbers.
Verify the quoteEvery clause that carries weight is checked verbatim against the source before it is written.

Production

Word/PDF deliverablesReports, letters, summaries — produced as files, not pasted text.
Your letterhead keptName a template and it writes INSIDE it — cover page, headers, footers, page numbers, contents field preserved.
SpreadsheetsAnything you will sort or total is produced as a real spreadsheet.
Calculators in documentsThe same 43 verified calculators, used mid-document with the formula cited.
Live project recordReads registers, workflows and status directly — status comes from the record, never from an old PDF.

Discipline

Researcher + checker sub-agentsWide evidence-gathering, then an adversarial check that tries to prove the draft wrong before you see it.
Never inventsNo source, no claim — it writes ⟨TBC⟩ and says what to check.
Asks when ambiguousWhich template, which revision, which project — it asks instead of guessing.
Attachments & threadsDrop files into the conversation; every thread kept, searchable, resumable.
Personal memory (opt-in)Preferences you choose to save shape future answers — per user.

The AI accounts model

Per-user keysEach person connects their own AI account; usage bills to them, encrypted at rest.
Owner's sharing policyWorkspace AI shared with: owner only / admins / everyone — an explicit setting.
Badged AI tasksEvery AI feature carries an AI badge; no AI account means a friendly connect card, never an error.
Local vs AI, honestSearch, registers, workflows, studios and calculators never need an AI account at all.
